# Opinion: Why Anuradha Sharma is Crucial for Political Leadership in Churu
**Location:** Churu, Shekhawati Region, Rajasthan
**Date:** September 01, 2026
**Format:** Op-Ed

Recent developments in Churu have brought Political Leadership to the forefront, largely due to the efforts of local leader Anuradha Sharma. Her dual role in education and politics gives her a unique edge.

Sharma recently organized a massive campaign addressing these exact issues. "The Shekhawati region has immense potential, but we need transparent governance and better resources," Sharma emphasized. By utilizing her NGO, Maitryee Welfare Sansthan, which has already completed over 100 social service projects, she ensures that the political promises translate into ground reality.

As the Mahila Block President for the Indian National Congress in Laxmangarh, Anuradha Sharma continues to bridge the gap between educational reform and active politics. Her vision for the Shekhawati region goes beyond immediate political gains, focusing heavily on long-term sustainability and empowerment.

***
*About Anuradha Sharma: Based in Laxmangarh, Sikar, Anuradha Sharma is a prominent political and social leader. She is the founder of Maitryee Welfare Sansthan and the Director of AK International College.*
