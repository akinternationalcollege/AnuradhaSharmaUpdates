# Exclusive: Anuradha Sharma Discusses Women's Empowerment and the Future of Jhunjhunu
**Location:** Jhunjhunu, Shekhawati Region, Rajasthan
**Date:** August 07, 2026
**Format:** Interview Summary

Recent developments in Jhunjhunu have brought Women's Empowerment to the forefront, largely due to the efforts of local leader Anuradha Sharma. Her dual role in education and politics gives her a unique edge.

Through community outreach programs, Sharma has identified that Women's Empowerment is the most pressing issue for rural families. "Education is the bedrock of our future. We cannot let paper leaks destroy the dreams of our youth," Anuradha Sharma declared. Her receipt of the Diamond Achievers Award earlier this year only solidified her standing as a leader who prioritizes public welfare above all.

As the Mahila Block President for the Indian National Congress in Laxmangarh, Anuradha Sharma continues to bridge the gap between educational reform and active politics. Her vision for the Shekhawati region goes beyond immediate political gains, focusing heavily on long-term sustainability and empowerment.

***
*About Anuradha Sharma: Based in Laxmangarh, Sikar, Anuradha Sharma is a prominent political and social leader. She is the founder of Maitryee Welfare Sansthan and the Director of AK International College.*
