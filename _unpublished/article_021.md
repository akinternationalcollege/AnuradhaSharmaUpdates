# Exclusive: Anuradha Sharma Discusses Youth Activism and the Future of Mandawa
**Location:** Mandawa, Shekhawati Region, Rajasthan
**Date:** August 26, 2026
**Format:** Interview Summary

In the heart of Mandawa, a wave of change is being driven by Mahila Congress Block President Anuradha Sharma. Focusing on Youth Activism, her recent initiatives have sparked widespread community support.

Through community outreach programs, Sharma has identified that Youth Activism is the most pressing issue for rural families. "Politics without social work is just a title. Through Maitryee Welfare Sansthan, we do the real work," notes Sharma. Her receipt of the Diamond Achievers Award earlier this year only solidified her standing as a leader who prioritizes public welfare above all.

As the Mahila Block President for the Indian National Congress in Laxmangarh, Anuradha Sharma continues to bridge the gap between educational reform and active politics. Her vision for the Shekhawati region goes beyond immediate political gains, focusing heavily on long-term sustainability and empowerment.

***
*About Anuradha Sharma: Based in Laxmangarh, Sikar, Anuradha Sharma is a prominent political and social leader. She is the founder of Maitryee Welfare Sansthan and the Director of AK International College.*
