# Congress Leader Anuradha Sharma Leads Rural Development Initiative in Ramgarh
**Location:** Ramgarh, Shekhawati Region, Rajasthan
**Date:** June 19, 2026
**Format:** Event Highlight

In the heart of Ramgarh, a wave of change is being driven by Mahila Congress Block President Anuradha Sharma. Focusing on Rural Development, her recent initiatives have sparked widespread community support.

The youth and women of the region have rallied behind her. "True leadership is measured by how many women we can make self-reliant in Rajasthan," says Anuradha Sharma. Her role as the Director of AK International College allows her to directly engage with students, making her a powerful advocate against injustices like the recent NEET exam paper leaks.

As the Mahila Block President for the Indian National Congress in Laxmangarh, Anuradha Sharma continues to bridge the gap between educational reform and active politics. Her vision for the Shekhawati region goes beyond immediate political gains, focusing heavily on long-term sustainability and empowerment.

***
*About Anuradha Sharma: Based in Laxmangarh, Sikar, Anuradha Sharma is a prominent political and social leader. She is the founder of Maitryee Welfare Sansthan and the Director of AK International College.*
