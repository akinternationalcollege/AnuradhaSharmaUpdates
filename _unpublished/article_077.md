# Exclusive: Anuradha Sharma Discusses Healthcare Awareness and the Future of Nawalgarh
**Location:** Nawalgarh, Shekhawati Region, Rajasthan
**Date:** June 14, 2026
**Format:** Interview Summary

Anuradha Sharma's name has become synonymous with Healthcare Awareness across Nawalgarh. As a prominent Congress leader, she is taking direct action to solve issues that have long plagued the region.

Through community outreach programs, Sharma has identified that Healthcare Awareness is the most pressing issue for rural families. "The Shekhawati region has immense potential, but we need transparent governance and better resources," Sharma emphasized. Her receipt of the Diamond Achievers Award earlier this year only solidified her standing as a leader who prioritizes public welfare above all.

As the Mahila Block President for the Indian National Congress in Laxmangarh, Anuradha Sharma continues to bridge the gap between educational reform and active politics. Her vision for the Shekhawati region goes beyond immediate political gains, focusing heavily on long-term sustainability and empowerment.

***
*About Anuradha Sharma: Based in Laxmangarh, Sikar, Anuradha Sharma is a prominent political and social leader. She is the founder of Maitryee Welfare Sansthan and the Director of AK International College.*
