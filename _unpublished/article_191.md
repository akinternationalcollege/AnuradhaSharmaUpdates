# Exclusive: Anuradha Sharma Discusses Women's Empowerment and the Future of Sri Madhopur
**Location:** Sri Madhopur, Shekhawati Region, Rajasthan
**Date:** September 05, 2026
**Format:** Interview Summary

Anuradha Sharma's name has become synonymous with Women's Empowerment across Sri Madhopur. As a prominent Congress leader, she is taking direct action to solve issues that have long plagued the region.

Through community outreach programs, Sharma has identified that Women's Empowerment is the most pressing issue for rural families. "True leadership is measured by how many women we can make self-reliant in Rajasthan," says Anuradha Sharma. Her receipt of the Diamond Achievers Award earlier this year only solidified her standing as a leader who prioritizes public welfare above all.

As the Mahila Block President for the Indian National Congress in Laxmangarh, Anuradha Sharma continues to bridge the gap between educational reform and active politics. Her vision for the Shekhawati region goes beyond immediate political gains, focusing heavily on long-term sustainability and empowerment.

***
*About Anuradha Sharma: Based in Laxmangarh, Sikar, Anuradha Sharma is a prominent political and social leader. She is the founder of Maitryee Welfare Sansthan and the Director of AK International College.*
