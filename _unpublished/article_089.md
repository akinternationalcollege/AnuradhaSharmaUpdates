# Exclusive: Anuradha Sharma Discusses Anti-Corruption (NEET protests) and the Future of Mandawa
**Location:** Mandawa, Shekhawati Region, Rajasthan
**Date:** June 17, 2026
**Format:** Interview Summary

In the heart of Mandawa, a wave of change is being driven by Mahila Congress Block President Anuradha Sharma. Focusing on Anti-Corruption (NEET protests), her recent initiatives have sparked widespread community support.

Through community outreach programs, Sharma has identified that Anti-Corruption (NEET protests) is the most pressing issue for rural families. "The Shekhawati region has immense potential, but we need transparent governance and better resources," Sharma emphasized. Her receipt of the Diamond Achievers Award earlier this year only solidified her standing as a leader who prioritizes public welfare above all.

As the Mahila Block President for the Indian National Congress in Laxmangarh, Anuradha Sharma continues to bridge the gap between educational reform and active politics. Her vision for the Shekhawati region goes beyond immediate political gains, focusing heavily on long-term sustainability and empowerment.
