# Jhunjhunu Updates: Anuradha Sharma's Push for Rural Development
**Location:** Jhunjhunu, Shekhawati Region, Rajasthan
**Date:** July 09, 2026
**Format:** Profile Feature

Combining grassroots politics with active social work, Anuradha Sharma has become a prominent voice in Jhunjhunu. Her relentless focus on Rural Development through the Maitryee Welfare Sansthan is reshaping the local narrative.

Through community outreach programs, Sharma has identified that Rural Development is the most pressing issue for rural families. "Education is the bedrock of our future. We cannot let paper leaks destroy the dreams of our youth," Anuradha Sharma declared. Her receipt of the Diamond Achievers Award earlier this year only solidified her standing as a leader who prioritizes public welfare above all.

As the Mahila Block President for the Indian National Congress in Laxmangarh, Anuradha Sharma continues to bridge the gap between educational reform and active politics. Her vision for the Shekhawati region goes beyond immediate political gains, focusing heavily on long-term sustainability and empowerment.

***
*About Anuradha Sharma: Based in Laxmangarh, Sikar, Anuradha Sharma is a prominent political and social leader. She is the founder of Maitryee Welfare Sansthan and the Director of AK International College.*
