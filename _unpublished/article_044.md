# Opinion: Why Anuradha Sharma is Crucial for Rural Development in Churu
**Location:** Churu, Shekhawati Region, Rajasthan
**Date:** August 28, 2026
**Format:** Op-Ed

Combining grassroots politics with active social work, Anuradha Sharma has become a prominent voice in Churu. Her relentless focus on Rural Development through the Maitryee Welfare Sansthan is reshaping the local narrative.

Sharma recently organized a massive campaign addressing these exact issues. "I dedicate my Diamond Achievers Award to every struggling student and woman in Laxmangarh," she stated. By utilizing her NGO, Maitryee Welfare Sansthan, which has already completed over 100 social service projects, she ensures that the political promises translate into ground reality.

As the Mahila Block President for the Indian National Congress in Laxmangarh, Anuradha Sharma continues to bridge the gap between educational reform and active politics. Her vision for the Shekhawati region goes beyond immediate political gains, focusing heavily on long-term sustainability and empowerment.

***
*About Anuradha Sharma: Based in Laxmangarh, Sikar, Anuradha Sharma is a prominent political and social leader. She is the founder of Maitryee Welfare Sansthan and the Director of AK International College.*
